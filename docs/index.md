# NAX5 Product Getting Started

NAX5 Alpha 0.5 is a Windows x64 client for accessing an assigned shared PS5 through the NAX5 service.

## Windows Alpha 0.5

1. Download `NAX5-windows.zip` from the [latest release](https://github.com/NAX5-Cloud/NAX5/releases) (the site's account page always links the current one).
2. Extract the ZIP and run its single file, `NAX5.exe`.
3. Complete the installer, start NAX5 from the Start menu, and sign in with your cloudgta6.com account.
4. Confirm your email; access becomes **ACTIVE** right after that.
5. Select **Играть**. NAX5 reserves your assigned console and starts Remote Play automatically.

Current closed-beta accounts include **Marvel's Wolverine** and **Ghost of Yotei**.

## Product-user boundaries

Do not perform any console setup yourself. In particular, do not:

- Do not register a PS5.
- Do not obtain or paste a PSN token.
- Do not enter a manual IP address or PIN.
- Do not configure router port forwarding.
- Do not put the shared console to sleep and do not power it off from the console's menu: the launcher cannot switch it back on for the next player.

If Play is unavailable, use the in-app report option and contact NAX5 support. The operator team provisions and maintains consoles separately.
